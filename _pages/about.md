I'm a network engineer based in Gurgaon, India, with a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Deenbandhu Chhotu Ram University of Science and Technology (DCRUST), Murthal. Most recently I worked as a Junior Network Analyst at GuestTek Interactive Entertainment, building and operating the network infrastructure behind multi-property hospitality deployments for operators such as Marriott and Hilton.

Research interests
------
My interests lie in **network security and privacy**, **networking architecture and protocol design**, and **applied cryptography**, particularly how cryptographic and access-control mechanisms behave once they are deployed in working networks.

Operations work has given me a practitioner's view of these questions. At GuestTek I segmented guest, staff and management traffic with VLANs, wrote access control lists that restricted inter-VLAN reachability and management-plane access, prioritised voice traffic with QoS on shared uplinks, and diagnosed connectivity faults on live properties using packet capture, interface counters and device logs. I also built internal monitoring over the Cisco Meraki and Aruba Central APIs, placing both vendors behind a single interface that reconciled their differing data models, authentication schemes and error behaviour.

During my undergraduate studies I took electives in Network Security & Cryptography, Information Security & Data Hiding, and blockchain technology. My team was runner-up among 40 teams at the national-level Smart India Hackathon 2023, working on the problem statement *Blockchain-Based Academic Certificate Validation*.
